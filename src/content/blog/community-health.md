## What's happening

MabEcare Foundation is hosting a community health outreach event this month. The day will include free basic health screenings, a donations drop-off point for families in need, and activities for children.

## Why it matters

Access to basic health checks is something many families in our community go without. This event is a small step toward closing that gap, run entirely by volunteers and supported by donations like yours.

## How you can help

- Volunteer on the day
- Donate items ahead of the event
- Simply show up and spread the word

We'll share more details, including the exact time and location, as the date gets closer.
