## Equip a Labor Ward, Save a Mother

Sokode Bagble Health Centre is a vital community clinic located in the Ho Municipal District of the Volta Region, Ghana. Serving over eight surrounding villages, the health centre’s modest maternity ward welcomes dozens of newborns each month. 

However, the clinic operates under severe resource constraints. Due to a lack of basic clinical equipment and maternal sanitization supplies, expecting mothers are often requested to purchase and bring their own surgical gloves, delivery sheets, and bleach before they can be admitted. To close this gap and ensure safe, hygienic, and dignified deliveries, **MabEcare Foundation** is launching an urgent maternal care donation drive.

---

We invite all community members, corporate organizations, and international partners to donate both material supplies and financial resources to equip the clinic's labor ward.

- **Date:** Saturday, August 29, 2026
- **Drop-off Point:** MabEcare Foundation Office, Ho (Opposite Municipal Hospital)
- **Distribution Venue:** Sokode Bagble Health Centre, Volta Region, Ghana
- **Time:** 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM GMT

---

## Critical Supplies Needed

We are accepting donations for the following items, grouped by clinical and maternal priority:

### 1. Maternal Delivery Kits (Urgent)
- Sterile surgical gloves (sizes 7, 7.5, and 8)
- Disposable delivery sheets (underpads)
- Cord clamps and sterile surgical blades
- Antiseptic liquids (Dettol, Savlon) and bleach (Parazone)

### 2. Newborn Care Kits
- Soft cotton baby swaddles and receiver blankets
- Newborn-sized diapers and baby wipes
- Infant hats, socks, and cotton onesies
- Gentle baby soaps and petroleum jelly

### 3. Ward Infrastructure
- New clinic bedsheets (standard single size, light blue/white)
- Reusable mackintosh mattress covers
- Digital blood pressure monitors and thermometers
