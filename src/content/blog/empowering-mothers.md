## The Ripple Effect of Investing in Women

When you educate and empower a mother, you do not just change one life — you lift an entire household, neighborhood, and generation out of poverty. Numerous global studies show that women reinvest up to **90% of their earnings** back into their families' education, nutrition, and healthcare. In contrast, men typically reinvest only 35% to 40%.

At MabEcare Foundation, we have experienced this firsthand. Economic independence is the single most powerful tool to help vulnerable mothers escape precarious living situations, secure nutritious meals for their babies, and afford life-saving healthcare.

---

## Our Skills Training Pillars

Our vocational skills programme is designed specifically for low-income mothers, teen mothers, and young widows who face severe economic barriers. We focus on training in three highly-marketable, sustainable trades that can be run directly from home with minimal starting capital:

- **Baking and Pastry Making** — Teaching techniques for bread-making, local pastries, and cake decorating to supply local markets, schools, and events.
- **Soap and Detergent Manufacturing** — Creating organic bar soaps, liquid dishwashing detergents, and floor cleaning agents that are in constant demand.
- **Textiles and Tailoring** — Developing skills in dressmaking, pattern drafting, and bag-making to encourage local trade and entrepreneurship.

## Real Success: Meet Ama

Before joining the MabEcare skills cohort, Ama was a single mother of two living in an underserved suburb in Ho. Without a formal education or stable job, she struggled to provide one meal a day for her children. 

```text
"I felt completely hopeless. Every day was a fight to survive."
- Ama, Skills Graduate
```

Through our 12-week soap-making course, Ama learned not only how to manufacture high-quality liquid detergent but also basic bookkeeping, marketing, and cost calculation. Today, Ama runs a small but thriving soap business. She now supplies three local boarding schools and two health centers, generating a stable income that pays for her children’s school fees and fresh meals.

## Empowering through Financial Literacy

Learning a physical craft is only half the battle. To ensure long-term, independent success, our program integrates extensive business development sessions:

1. **Micro-Savings Groups (VSLAs)** — Setting up local community-based savings pools that allow mothers to borrow micro-loans at negligible interest rates to purchase machinery.
2. **Branding & Packaging** — Teaching basic labeling standards, sanitary storage, and professional presentation techniques to help them charge fair prices.
3. **Digital Marketing Basics** — Empowering women to leverage WhatsApp and community networks to market their services and take orders ahead of production.
