Hi! I put this together so you can write and publish your own posts whenever you're ready, without needing me to do it for you.

## Writing a new post

I want you to start by opening the repository on GitHub, then going to `src/content/blog/`. From there, click "Add file," then "Create new file," and give it a name related to your post, something like `event-name-2026.md`.

For the top part of the file, I'd like you to copy the same format from the `introduction.md` file already in that folder. Just fill in your own title, a short description, the category, and any tags that fit. Then write your post underneath.

While you're still working on it, leave `draft` set to `true`. That keeps it hidden from the live site until you're ready.

## Publishing it

Once you're happy with the post, I want you to change `draft` to `false`.

Then scroll to the bottom of the page. You'll see a section for committing your changes, just click "Commit changes" there. You don't need to touch anything else.

That's the whole process. Once you commit, the website updates on its own, usually within a minute or two.

## A few things to keep in mind

I want you to remember you'll need a GitHub account with access to the project, which I can set up for you if you don't have one yet.

If you ever want to take a post down without deleting it, just come back and set `draft` back to `true`, then commit again.

If anything feels confusing at any point, reach out and I'll walk you through it.
