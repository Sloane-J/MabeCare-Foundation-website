## What is albinism?

Albinism is a genetic condition caused by changes in the genes that make melanin. Melanin is what gives skin, hair, and eyes their colour.

Even so, cultural beliefs and misconceptions often lead to stigma and discrimination against people with albinism.

## Meet Stephanie

Stephanie Peni is 8 years old and lives with albinism. She has started to face skin and eye challenges that affect her learning.

To keep learning, she needs:

- Magnifying glasses
- Sunscreen
- Educational materials

Stephanie dreams of becoming a nurse. We believe her condition should never limit her dreams.

## How you can help

Together, we can make inclusion a reality for Stephanie.
